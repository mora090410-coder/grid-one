Fundraiser workflow

One board moves through named game-day horizons.

  • Fill: create the draft board and assign sold squares.
  • Reconcile: resolve duplicate public labels and acknowledge OPEN squares.
  • Draw: preview and commit axis digits.
  • Preview: inspect the exact viewer link before sharing.
  • Go Live: publish the board for score, winner, and scenario tracking.

Trust and recovery

The viewer sees the state of the record, not theater.

  • Score source, game period, and update timing are explicit.
  • Stale or manual states preserve known board information with recovery language.
  • Public board link excludes private purchaser email, seller attribution, and payment status.
  • Winner history resolves Q1, Q2, Q3, and Final without handling money.
